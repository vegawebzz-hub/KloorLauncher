**Currency:** Kloor Credits (`KLOOR_CREDIT`)  
**Version:** 2026-08-24

- Rewarded ads are optional, individually chosen, non-personalized, and restricted to users confirming they are at least 18.
- Refusing or withdrawing ad consent does not disable Microsoft login, installations, downloads, or Minecraft launching.

- Credits are issued only after a valid signed server-to-server completion event. Duplicate, forged, replayed, expired, or over-limit events receive no credit.
- Live ads remain disabled until a provider expressly approves the Windows/Electron or hosted-web flow.
- Shop Terms and Reward Rules are accepted separately from advertising consent before a new purchase.
- Every item has a fixed visible price. Purchases and balances use an auditable ledger and idempotent transactions.
- Current items are original permanent KloorLauncher themes. There are no random rewards, loot boxes, gameplay advantages, Minecraft capes/assets, paid servers, mods, or modpacks.
- Credits do not expire and cannot be bought for money in this version, sold, transferred, gifted, traded, cashed out, exchanged for crypto/gift cards, or used outside KloorLauncher. They are not Minecoins.

The publisher may reverse a demonstrably fraudulent or erroneous credit while preserving an audit record and explaining the decision.
